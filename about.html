About Life Saving Medical Center

Located in Wakiso Town Council, Life Saving Medical Center is dedicated to providing high-quality, accessible, and reliable medical services to our community. Operating 24/7, our facility is equipped with modern infrastructure and staffed by a highly qualified team of healthcare professionals.

The Lifesaving Health Care Foundation (LHCF) is a registered nonprofit community-based organization based in Wakiso District, Uganda. We are dedicated to bridging the gap in healthcare services for underserved populations, particularly focusing on vulnerable groups such as women, children, and young people. Our mission revolves around promoting access to affordable, high-quality healthcare services while empowering communities through education and advocacy.

Whether you visit us in person or connect via our virtual consultation platforms, our priority remains the same: saving lives and promoting long-term health with dignity and compassion.

Our Symbol & Motto

"Affordable Quality Health care is our Priority"

Our symbol, represented by the vibrant water droplet and the human figure in the logo, encapsulates our commitment to vitality, growth, and compassionate care.

Our Vision

A world where every individual has access to affordable and high-quality health care, free from social, economic, and gender-based disparities.

Our Mission

To deliver lifesaving health care and support systems to communities in need through high-quality medical services, community empowerment, and sustainable partnerships.

Our Core Values

Accessibility

Making health care services affordable and within reach for all.

Compassion

Providing care that prioritizes dignity and respect. We treat every patient with maximum empathy, kindness, and deep respect.

Sustainability

Building systems that ensure long-term community impact.

Empowerment

Educating and advocating for healthier lives.

Innovation

Continuously improving service delivery with modern tools and strategies.

Excellence & Integrity

We maintain the highest professional standards, clinical ethics, honesty, and transparency in all procedures and diagnostics.
