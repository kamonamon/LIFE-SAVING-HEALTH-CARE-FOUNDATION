Foundation Initiatives

School Health Outreach Program

Through the Life Saving Medical Care Foundation, our fully qualified medical teams are invited directly into schools to administer foundational health checks for students across all classes. We assess physical development, complete diagnostic evaluations, and target basic illnesses to build healthier classrooms.

Early detection keeps children safe, concentrated, and active in school. Our foundation makes these on-site checks simple, seamless, and completely safe.
